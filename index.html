03 / About me

From game design to the last shader pass.

I've always been super into computer games and how they bring visuals to life. It's crazy cool how real-world stuff can be turned into virtual awesomeness.

So I've been lucky to dive deep into game development, working on a bunch of projects – from dreaming up game designs, crafting 3D models and building whole worlds, to adding special effects, making custom shaders and jazzing things up with post-processing.

Oh, and I've even gotten into the nitty-gritty of making things run smoothly and getting stuff all set up for marketing. It's been quite a ride!
